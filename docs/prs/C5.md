## Build

- FailurePolicy (no_std, Copy): base 50 ms; cap default 5 s and at most 8 s (dev accepts 10 s; the design cap of 8 s wins, see report); backoff_after 3; auto_off_after 0, which means never. Construction rejects a cap above 8 s.
- Backoff per step: from the backoff_after-th failure in a row, the delay is base * 2^(n - after), capped. The step is admitted again when the clock passes until. The clock is read only for a step in backoff. A success resets the count.
- Containment: a Fault does not wake consumers and commits the inputs (the tick is dropped). The tick goes on. fail_fast (on in tests and CI) ends the tick with RunError::Fault.
- Recovery per step (from A8 metadata): Fail (default), SkipTick, Retry{max}, Restart. Retry calls the body again in the same tick with the inputs still in place, up to max, then fails. Restart sets the RESTART flag in StepCx for the next call; the body resets its own state. Needs the StepCx flag.
- Manual off and on: a per-step DISABLED state set by the host. A disabled step is not called, its inputs are dropped, and its consumers see nothing. Switching on clears the backoff at once and keeps the failure record.
- Auto-off: only when auto_off_after > 0. After that many failures in a row the step is disabled with an auto flag. Switching it on clears the flag. Off by default.
- InputDropped from a body is Skipped, not a Fault, and is counted apart from failures.
- Panics: under std the host wraps each call in catch_unwind and turns a panic into a Fault. no_std panics abort; nothing in the runner catches them.
- Do not carry the string-carrying NodeError variants or the load-time handler errors (missing handler, external handler). Those become load errors in B8 and D.

## Proof

core/runner/tests/failure.rs ports the dev tests with plain-function steps: default contains a failure and keeps ticking; fail_fast ends the run; skip_tick drops the output and keeps running; retry reruns on the same inputs; exhausted retries fail like Fail; a failing node's ticks after the first failure still run (one failed tick does not silence the ticks after it); the third failure starts the backoff at base; the backoff delay never exceeds 8 s on an injected clock over 100 consecutive failures; a failing node is never switched off by default; manual off runs no tick and its consumer sees nothing.
