## Build

- BackpressureStrategy from core/policy.rs: None, BoundedQueues, ErrorOnOverflow. The planner (B) sets it per edge. The runner reads only the per-edge flags.
- Blocking edge: a step whose blocking output slot is full is not called. Its inputs stay queued and it goes to carry, so it is retried next tick. Each deferral is counted (backpressure_deferrals).
- BoundedQueues: drop and reject incoming values with an event, without blocking. ErrorOnOverflow: a Full push is an Overflow error, and fail_fast ends the tick.
- A merged region is blocked when any of its boundary outputs is blocked.

## Proof

core/runner/tests/backpressure.rs: a blocking producer is deferred, not dropped (ported from resilience.rs blocking_backpressure_defers_the_producer_instead_of_dropping); a bounded fan-out drops with an event; ErrorOnOverflow fails fast; no step is called while its blocking output is full (counter).
