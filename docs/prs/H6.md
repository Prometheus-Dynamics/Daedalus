## Build

- Files only; no code. Dev has no packaging for the daemon.
- daedalusd.socket gives socket activation on the same unix socket path H4 uses by default. daedalusd.service uses that socket, runs as a dedicated user and has the hardening directives: ProtectSystem=strict, NoNewPrivileges, PrivateTmp, RuntimeDirectory.
- Plugin directory under /usr/lib/daedalusd/plugins; config at /etc/daedalusd/daedalusd.toml.
- check.sh runs systemd-analyze verify on the units and daedalusd --check-config on the sample config, and checks that the socket path matches H4's default.

## Proof

check.sh passes in CI on the hosted Linux runner: systemd-analyze verify on both units, and daedalusd --check-config on the sample config.

## Status

Done. The six files are in `service/daedalusd/packaging/`, and `scripts/ci.d/packaging.sh` runs `check.sh` as a gate step. Nothing was installed, enabled or started on the machine this was written on: the check verifies copies of the units in a temp directory.

As built:

- **`daedalusd.socket`** listens on `/run/daedalusd/daedalusd.sock`, the daemon's default, owned by the `daedalusd` user and group, mode 0660.
- **`daedalusd.service`** requires that socket, runs `/usr/bin/daedalusd --config /etc/daedalusd/daedalusd.toml` as `daedalusd`, and has `ProtectSystem=strict`, `NoNewPrivileges`, `PrivateTmp`, `RuntimeDirectory=daedalusd`, and also `PrivateDevices`, `ProtectHome`, the kernel and control group protections, `RestrictAddressFamilies` (unix and IP, for a loopback listener), `RestrictNamespaces`, `RestrictSUIDSGID` and `LockPersonality`. The failure rule is in the unit: `Restart=on-failure` with the delay stepping from 1 s to at most 8 s, and `StartLimitIntervalSec=0` so the manager never turns the unit off for restarting too often. `systemctl stop` is the manual off.
- **`daedalusd.toml`** is the sample config: that socket, `/usr/lib/daedalusd/plugins`, 64 runs, 64 connections, `info`.
- **`sysusers.conf`** makes the user. **`tmpfiles.conf`** makes `/etc/daedalusd`, `/usr/lib/daedalusd/plugins` and `/run/daedalusd`.
- **`check.sh`** runs `systemd-analyze verify` on both units and fails on any message; runs `daedalusd --check-config` on the sample config; and checks that the socket unit's path is the one the config resolves to, the daemon's `DEFAULT_SOCKET`, and what the daemon resolves to under the service's `RuntimeDirectory`; that the service reads the daemon's `DEFAULT_CONFIG` as a dedicated user that `sysusers.conf` makes and that owns the socket; that the plugin and config directories are the ones `tmpfiles.conf` makes; and that the hardening and restart directives are present.

Differences from the brief:

- **The units are verified as copies** whose `ExecStart` names the daemon cargo just built, since `/usr/bin/daedalusd` is not installed where the check runs. `DAEDALUSD=<path>` checks another build.
- **`tmpfiles.conf` also makes `/run/daedalusd`**, owned by the service user. The socket unit would otherwise create it owned by root, and a client in the `daedalusd` group could not reach the socket before the daemon's first start.
- **`RestartSteps` and `RestartMaxDelaySec`** need systemd 254 or newer. An older one ignores them with a warning, and restarts every second.
- **Not run:** the units were never loaded by a service manager. A socket passed the way the socket unit passes it was tested in H4, with `systemd-socket-activate`.
