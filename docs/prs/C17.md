## Build

- Synchronized: frames are grouped by stamp into atomic ticks. A group that stalls resolves at its timeout with the partial policy. A skipped frame resolves without waiting. Per-camera clock offsets are applied.
- Independent: each camera ticks alone, and a frame older than max_age is dropped.
- Out-of-order frames of one camera are sorted. A full buffer resolves the oldest group first.
- The deadline checks belong to the drive loop (C20), not the tick.
