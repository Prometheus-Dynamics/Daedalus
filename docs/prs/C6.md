## Build

- Behind features std and threads. Without them the module is absent and the serial runner is the only runner.
- Lanes come from the plan: each step has a lane index (B7 and B9 fill it). A step is ready when it is dirty and its unfinished producer count is zero. Ready steps go to one shared queue of fixed capacity (the step count), ordered by priority (NODE_PRIORITY_META_KEY: higher first, index order among equals).
- Workers come from a pool made once at host start. A tick fans out to the pool. No thread is spawned and nothing is allocated per tick.
- Slot access between lanes: a slot is written only by its producer step and read only by its consumer steps, and the dependency counts order them. Slot metadata is protected under std (a lock per slot, or atomics, as A5 decides).
- Result equality: a parallel tick gives the same outputs, counters and fault set as the serial tick on the same inputs.
- A panic in a worker is a Fault of that step and ends the tick, as C5 says.
- Do not carry: the segment DAG with its per-run queue objects, the per-worker executor snapshots.

## Proof

core/runner/tests/lanes.rs: serial and parallel ticks match on 200 seeded random plans of plain-function steps (outputs, counters, faults); the parallel invariants (chain, branch, bounded fan-out, independent failure, first failing, many failures) and the priority order test ported from dev; zero allocations per warm parallel tick after the pool is warm (counting allocator).
