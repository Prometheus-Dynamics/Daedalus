## Build

- InboundWaiter: a core::future::Future (runtime agnostic) with wakers stored under the buffer lock and woken after the lock is released. Blocking wait on a Condvar under the threads feature.
- inbound_fd (Linux, std): an eventfd a host can poll next to its own descriptors. The protocol is the dev one: wait until readable, clear, take pending input, then rearm. A push signals only when the fd is not already signalled.
- Drive loops: tick_until_idle, tick_ready, drive_blocking, and drive with a stop handle that wakes waiters. The host lock is never held while a tick or an output callback runs.
- Stop handles keep their dev semantics: stop sets the flag and wakes the waiter; reset clears it for reuse.

## Proof

Port host_graph_poll.rs (one poll drives a camera fd and the graph's inbound fd) and the drive tests of host_graph_events.rs (tick on input waits for feed; drive_blocking processes input until stopped; it exits when the bridge closes) to core/host/tests/drive.rs.
