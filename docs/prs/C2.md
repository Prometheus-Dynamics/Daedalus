## Build

- A pending list of (step, fence) entries, sized at load to the number of steps with may_pend. Registering an entry never allocates.
- Each tick, after the idle check, poll every registered fence through the poll entry of A3's Fence. Ready: remove it and wake meta.wake of its step. Pending: keep it. Failed: remove it and send that step through the C5 fault path with no wake.
- A pending step's inputs were committed when it returned Pending. Its consumers wait for Ready. The step is not called again until its fence completes.
- Ready fences are processed in step index order, so the same set of completions gives the same tick.
- Dependency on A3: Fence must carry a poll entry point and Status must have Ready, Pending and Failed. If A3 lacks this, stop and report to the A-group owner. Do not edit A3 here.
- Cancelling a pending fence belongs to C7.

## Proof

Tests with fake fences that become ready after k polls: consumers are not woken before Ready; ready fences wake in index order; one Failed fence faults only its own step; registration and polling make zero allocations (counting allocator).
