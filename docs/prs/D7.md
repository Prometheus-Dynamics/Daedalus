## Build

- Extend examples/chip_blink (D5, compiled mode) with the other modes. bin/tunable is the compiled graph with the marked constants as parameters (the 4 parameters of graph.json). bin/loaded loads plan A from the blob writer (D3) and runs it through the interpreter.
- Fan-in graph (graph_fanin.json): the blink nodes plus the command_active conversion. It runs in all three modes natively. The conversion is a registered conversion node (A8, B4), not an attribute.
- generated/ holds the checked-in output of the Source form (D4) and the two plan blobs (D3). examples/generate.rs regenerates them. tests/generated.rs fails when they are stale.
- The native tests use a counting allocator in tests/common, and check that every mode matches the node functions with zero allocations.
- scripts/ci.sh chip (D5) also builds the tunable and loaded firmwares for both thumb targets and prints their sizes. Budgets from dev: tunable at most 6 KiB flash and 512 B RAM; loaded at most 12 KiB flash and 584 B RAM (the design's upper bound). Measured sizes are added to docs/chips.md (D5's file).
- Dropped: examples/mcu_blink/tools/plan.rs (the B13 plan API and the D3 writer replace it).

## Proof

cargo test -p chip-blink (native): modes.rs (tunable_matches_compiled_then_follows_its_parameters, loaded_plan_matches_compiled_without_allocating, swaps_to_another_plan_at_run_time, rejects_invalid_blobs_and_keeps_the_running_plan). fanin.rs (plans_fit_the_firmware_arena_and_name_the_fan_in, compiled_fan_in_matches_the_node_functions_without_allocating, a_pushed_command_takes_precedence_over_the_threshold, tunable_fan_in_matches_compiled_and_follows_its_parameters, loaded_fan_in_plan_matches_compiled_and_plan_b_ignores_the_command, rejects_the_blob_format_of_another_version). native.rs and generated.rs (generated_files_match_the_planner). scripts/ci.sh chip on both thumb targets: all three modes within budget.
