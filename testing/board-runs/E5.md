# Board run: E5 one submit per region on V3D

Status: partly run on CM5 2026-11-10 (one region test fails on V3D; the batching timing misses dev's 1.32 ms).

## Check

Build from NVMe, never from the HDD.

- `DAEDALUS_GPU_REQUIRED=1 cargo test -p daedalus-wgpu --test region`: all four have to pass.
- Time a region of eight small ops (the add and triple shaders of that test, 1000 elements) from
  the step's call to its fence resolving, as the median of 200 runs after 20 warm-up runs.
  `dev` measured 1.32 ms on V3D with one submit, and 1.30 ms with eight. This must not be slower
  than 1.32 ms. Record the number, and the allocation counts the last test prints.

## Result


Run on 2026-11-10 on the CM5 (raze2): OS image 2026.2.0-dev.993+e1097ec7, kernel 7.2.9-v8-16k, aarch64, 4 cores, 4 GB RAM. V3D 7.1.10.2 through the Broadcom ICD (Vulkan 1.3.354, driver 26.1.8), `/dev/dri/renderD128`. Tested commit: `next` at bdbfe947.

Method: the test binaries were built with `cargo test --release --no-run --target aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu -p <crate>` on NVMe, copied to `/tmp/daedalus-next/bin` on the board, and run as `DAEDALUS_GPU_REQUIRED=1 flock /run/lock/raze-gpu.lock ./<binary> --nocapture --test-threads=1`, with `DAEDALUS_DMA_HEAP_REQUIRED=1` where the variable is named below. Logs are on the board under `/tmp/daedalus-next/logs`.

- `wgpu-region` (with `DAEDALUS_DMA_HEAP_REQUIRED=1`): 8 of 9 pass. The procedure names four tests; this branch has nine.
  - Pass: `a_lost_device_faults_the_region_then_is_reopened_and_the_region_runs_again`, `a_raw_submitter_is_made_on_the_backends_device`, `a_recording_body_records_into_the_regions_one_submit`, `a_region_keeps_what_it_read_until_its_next_run`, `a_region_steps_allocations_dont_grow_with_its_nodes`, `a_value_read_inside_and_outside_the_region_is_one_buffer`, `eight_nodes_run_as_one_submit_and_eight_passes`, `what_cannot_run_is_refused_or_faults_and_names_the_node`.
  - FAIL: `a_uniform_takes_plain_bytes_or_bytes_already_on_the_device` at `backends/wgpu/tests/region.rs:492:5`: left `[1, 2, 3, 4]`, right `[5, 10, 15, 20]`. The inline-bytes uniform of 5 gave an output that scales by 1 on V3D. The device-resident half of the test is after this assertion and did not run. Not diagnosed; this is a finding.
- Timing: `daedalus-gpu-budgets batching` (the 8-op region that I2 names for this figure), three runs each. One submit: 2.885, 2.897 and 2.890 ms. Eight submits: 3.100, 3.140 and 3.118 ms. The procedure's bound is 1.32 ms for one submit: this is a miss, at 2.2 times `dev`'s figure. dev's eight-submit figure is 1.30 ms; this is 2.4 times it. The tool's region is the one I2 names, not the procedure's own eight-shader region, and the miss has not been diagnosed.
- Allocations, from `daedalus-gpu-budgets allocation` (warm run): 32 for 1 node, 43 for 2, 62 for 4 and 102 for 8. The region test prints no counts.
