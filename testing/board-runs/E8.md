## Timings, 2026-11-11

Build: this branch, based on `origin/next` at 9039013d. Three CM5 runs (OS image 2026.2.0-dev.993+e1097ec7, V3D 7.1.10.2), `daedalus-gpu-budgets pixels busy cached heap-read` under `flock /run/lock/raze-gpu.lock`. Each figure is a median of 50 after 5 warm-up runs, and the ranges are the lowest and highest of the three runs.

The busy answer: a shader with one run in flight answers the next run `Busy`, and `shader.run` returns at once. `daedalus-gpu-budgets busy` times 1000 such calls per round, over 55 rounds, and takes the median per call.

- Time per `Busy` answer: 1.219 to 1.246 us per call (median of 50).
- Calls answered `Busy`: 54945 of 55000 in each run, so the answer is nearly every call while the run is in flight.

No dev figure is quoted for the busy answer; the procedure asks for the time of the answer, which is what a step pays when the device is full. The `is_done` figures from the earlier fences section (0.037 us on a finished ticket, 0.87 to 1.04 us on a pending one) are unchanged.
