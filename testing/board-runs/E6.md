- `DAEDALUS_GPU_REQUIRED=1 cargo test -p daedalus-wgpu-author --test pixel`: all eight have to

  the median of 200 after 20 warm-up runs. It must be one submit and one pass, and cost no more
  than the single box 3x3 fragment pass (`dev` 2.83 ms) plus the arithmetic of the extra nodes.
- The same eight nodes as the merged compute form through a region, and as eight unmerged
  regions. Record all three numbers: `dev` measured storage-buffer compute at 36.4 ms against
  2.83 ms for the fragment pass on a box 3x3, which is why V3D is meant to get the fragment form.
