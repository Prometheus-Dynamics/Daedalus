# Board run: E17 dma-buf blocks on the CM5

Status: untested on board. Run by a local session on the CM5 (Raze), only on the maintainer's
word.

## Check

Build from NVMe, never from the HDD.

- `cargo test -p daedalus-dmabuf -- --nocapture`: nothing may print `skipped`.
- With `Source::Heap("system")`: take and drop a 1 MiB block 1000 times after a warm-up, and
  record that the counting allocator sees no allocation and the pool's `allocated` stays at 1.
- Time, as the median of 50, reading back a 1 MiB and a 16 MiB heap block through
  `block.access(Access::Read)`. `dev` measured 3.6 ms for 16 MiB from a dma-heap buffer and
  13.1 ms from V3D's own host-visible memory.
