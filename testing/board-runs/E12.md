# Board run: E12 the timed GPU budgets on V3D

Status: run on CM5 2026-11-10 (the one-submit batching figure misses dev's 1.32 ms).

## Check

Build from NVMe, never from the HDD.

- `cargo run --release -p daedalus-gpu-budgets -- limits`: record the adapter and its limits.
- `cargo run --release -p daedalus-gpu-budgets -- batching`. Required: 8 ops in one submit at
  or below 1.32 ms, which is what `dev` measured on V3D (1.30 ms with 8 submits). Record both
  lines, and fill the CM5 column of the budget table in `docs/design.md`.
- `cargo run --release -p daedalus-gpu-budgets -- allocation`: the counts are the same on
  every device. Record them if they differ from 32, 43, 62 and 102 for 1, 2, 4 and 8 nodes.
- `cargo run --release -p daedalus-gpu-budgets -- dispatch-floor readback`: record each line.
- `DAEDALUS_GPU_REQUIRED=1 cargo test -p daedalus-wgpu-conformance -p daedalus-gpu-budgets`:
  the counted budgets hold on V3D as they do on lavapipe. A miss is a finding: record the
  measure and the count.

The RX 6800 XT row of the table (0.37 ms with 8 submits, 0.15 ms with 1) is not a board run.

## Result


Run on 2026-11-10 on the CM5 (raze2): OS image 2026.2.0-dev.993+e1097ec7, kernel 7.2.9-v8-16k, aarch64, 4 cores, 4 GB RAM. V3D 7.1.10.2 through the Broadcom ICD (Vulkan 1.3.354, driver 26.1.8), `/dev/dri/renderD128`. Tested commit: `next` at bdbfe947.

Method: the test binaries were built with `cargo test --release --no-run --target aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu -p <crate>` on NVMe, copied to `/tmp/daedalus-next/bin` on the board, and run as `DAEDALUS_GPU_REQUIRED=1 flock /run/lock/raze-gpu.lock ./<binary> --nocapture --test-threads=1`, with `DAEDALUS_DMA_HEAP_REQUIRED=1` where the variable is named below. Logs are on the board under `/tmp/daedalus-next/logs`.

- `daedalus-gpu-budgets limits`: adapter `V3D 7.1.10.2`; the limits are recorded in E1.
- `daedalus-gpu-budgets batching`, three runs. 8 ops in one submit: 2.885, 2.897 and 2.890 ms. 8 ops in eight submits: 3.100, 3.140 and 3.118 ms. The procedure requires one submit at or below 1.32 ms, which is a MISS (about 2.2 times dev's 1.32 ms). Eight submits: dev measured 1.30 ms; this is 2.4 times it. The miss is repeatable across the three runs and is not diagnosed.
- `daedalus-gpu-budgets allocation`: 32, 43, 62 and 102 allocations for 1, 2, 4 and 8 nodes, the same as the procedure's expected counts. A merged chain of 4 pixel bodies gives 33.
- `daedalus-gpu-budgets dispatch-floor`: one pass, submit and wait: 98.9 us.
- `daedalus-gpu-budgets readback`: read back 4 KiB 0.061 ms; 256 KiB 0.262 ms; 4096 KiB 3.450 ms; 64 KiB up and back: 1 upload, 1 download.
- `DAEDALUS_GPU_REQUIRED=1` tests: `conf-kernels` 2 of 2 pass; `conf-merge` 2 of 2 pass; `conf-regions` 5 of 5 pass, including `a_warm_step_allocates_within_its_budget_and_not_yet_nothing` and `the_backend_rules_hold`; `budgets-test` 5 of 5 pass. The counted budgets hold on V3D in these tests.
- The RX 6800 XT row is not a board run, and was not taken.
- docs/design.md has no CM5 column: its budget table has one column, `dev`. The table was not changed, since the procedure says to fill only a column that exists.

## Rerun of the 8-op timing, 2026-11-10

The 2.89 ms above was not the workload `dev` measured. The tool timed eight hex-encode ops, each writing twice its input, and waited after every submit. `dev` timed eight 64 KiB shader copies, waited for once. The tool now times that.

On the same workload, same board, release build, three runs: 8 submits 1.34 to 1.40 ms, 1 submit 1.37 to 1.42 ms. `dev` recorded 1.30 and 1.32 ms. **That is 3 to 8 percent over `dev`'s figures, and over the 1.32 ms this procedure requires.** `dev` was not re-run on this board, so how much of that is the board's state is not known.

Getting there took a fix: the kernel ops copied their counts into a uniform before every pass, a buffer copy between passes, which cost about 0.2 to 0.35 ms over eight ops on V3D (1.53 and 1.74 ms before it). The counts are now a small buffer bound directly, as `dev` had them.

For reference, not a board run: the same workload on an RX 6800 XT is 0.35 ms with 8 submits and 0.14 ms with 1, against `dev`'s 0.37 and 0.15.

## Rerun with one pass a region, 2026-11-10

Same board, release build, three runs of `gpu-budgets batching` (eight 64 KiB shader copies): 8 submits 1.37 to 1.40 ms, 1 submit 1.31 to 1.36 ms. `dev` recorded 1.30 and 1.32 ms. The one-submit figure is at `dev`'s: one run under it and two within 3 percent. One op submitted and waited for alone is 73 µs. Every test binary of the GPU crates passes on the board with this change.

## Rerun with regions recorded with Vulkan itself, 2026-11-10

Same board, release build, three runs of `gpu-budgets batching allocation dispatch-floor`.

- `allocation`: a region of 1, 2, 4 and 8 shader nodes allocates 0 times in a warm run, where it was 32, 34, 38 and 44. A merged chain of 4 pixel bodies: 0. One kernel op recorded and submitted alone is still 32, and eight in one batch 42: kernel ops are not regions and still go through wgpu's encoder.
- `batching` (kernel ops, so not changed by this): 8 submits 1.33 to 1.40 ms, 1 submit 1.31 to 1.37 ms.
- `dispatch-floor`: 74 to 79 µs.
- Every test binary of `daedalus-vk-sys`, `daedalus-wgpu`, `daedalus-gpu-budgets` and `daedalus-wgpu-conformance` passes on the board, `the_backend_rules_hold` with the allocation rule among them.
