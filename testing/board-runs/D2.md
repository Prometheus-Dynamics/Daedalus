# Board run: D2 CPU merging

Status: re-run 2026-11-11 on CM5 (raze2) at `next` 6245c925: per-stage times taken from the budget harness's chain runs. The instruction count was not taken (valgrind is not on the board). Run again 2026-11-16 in the batched session: instruction and cycle counts taken with `counters`; the per-stage wall time was not captured that session (see the 2026-11-16 result). The callgrind count is still not taken. The per-stage wall time and the ticks rows are taken in the next batch's ticks figure (run-sheet.md), and D2 closes on them. The PMU counts from `counters` stand in for callgrind, which is not on the board; they are aarch64 counts, not compared with dev's x86 callgrind 27,064.

## Check

- Per-stage overhead of a chain of CPU nodes, merged and unmerged (`Config { merge: false }`). `dev`
  measured 498 ns merged and 655 ns unmerged per stage.
- The 16-stage idle tick in instructions, against `dev`'s 27,064 (`origin/dev`). Commands: `daedalus-budget-harness chain` and `daedalus-budget-harness idle`, with `valgrind --tool=callgrind` for the instruction count.
- Merged must be below unmerged, and neither may be worse than `dev`. A number that gets worse is
  reported with the reason.

## Record

Commit, kernel, CPU governor, both per-stage times and the instruction count, next to the `dev`
figures above.

## Result


Run on 2026-11-10 on the CM5 (raze2): OS image 2026.2.0-dev.993+e1097ec7, kernel 7.2.9-v8-16k, aarch64, 4 cores, CPU governor `performance` at 2.4 GHz. Tested commit: `next` at bdbfe947.

From the budget harness's `ticks` bench (the C1 record above), per stage with one feed and one take per tick included:

| Form | CM5, `next` | Per stage | `dev` (CM5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| merged (`fused_chain_16_tick`) | 1.0741 µs | 67.1 ns | 498 ns |
| unmerged (`unfused_chain_16_tick`) | 1.8428 µs | 115.2 ns | 655 ns |

Merged is below unmerged on this board: yes. Neither figure is worse than the `dev` figure recorded for the CM5. `dev` was not re-run on this board, so this is a recorded comparison, not a verified one.

Instruction count of the 16-stage idle tick: not run. valgrind is not on the board, so the count (27,064 on `dev`) cannot be taken here. The idle tick's wall time on this board is 33.42 ns; that is not comparable to an instruction count.

## Result, 2026-11-11

Commit `6245c925`, raze2 (CM5, V3D 7.1.10.2). Commands: `./daedalus-budget-harness chain 2000000 16` (merged), `chain 2000000 16 unfused`, `chain 2000000 1`, and `idle 2000000`, each timed as wall clock (`/proc/uptime`, 10 ms resolution).

| Run | Wall time, 2,000,000 ticks | Per tick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 stages, merged | 2.23 s | 1.115 µs |
| 16 stages, unmerged | 3.92 s | 1.960 µs |
| 1 stage | 0.79 s | 0.395 µs |
| 16 stages, idle | 0.07 s | 0.035 µs |

Per stage, by the record's two methods: merged 69.7 ns (tick ÷ 16) or 48 ns ((16-stage tick − 1-stage tick) ÷ 15); unmerged 122.5 ns or 104 ns. Merged is below unmerged, as the record asks. The figures include one feed and one take a tick.

Not compared with dev's 498 ns (merged) and 655 ns (unmerged) on this line: dev measured a linear fit of its `graph_overhead` p50, a different measure. The instruction count (27,064 on dev) was not taken.

## Result, 2026-11-16 (batched session)

Run at `next` 748ef3cc on the CM5, three rounds. The counts come from `counters` (the `daedalus-counters`
crate, `perf_event_open`, user space). Each count per tick is the 1,100,000-tick run minus the
100,000-tick run, divided by 1,000,000. Every counter printed a value; none printed `na`.

| 16-stage tick (median of three rounds) | Instructions per tick | Cycles per tick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| idle (`budget-harness idle`) | 146 | 58 |
| chain, unfused (`chain ... unfused`, feed and take included) | 12,397 | 4,572 |
| chain, fused (`chain ... fused`) | 7,875 | 2,620 |

Merged is below unmerged on both counts. The instruction counts are aarch64 counts from the PMU. The
reference's 27,064 instructions for the idle tick is a callgrind count on x86, so the two are not
compared.

Not measured this session: per-stage wall time, merged and unmerged. The harness prints the tick count
and a checksum, not a time, and the step log's timestamps are whole seconds, too coarse for a
per-stage figure. The 2026-11-11 per-stage figures above stand as the last timed result.

## 2026-11-17 (second session)

Instruction counts are unchanged: 146 per idle tick, 12,397 unfused and 7,875 fused. The unfused and fused cycle counts stay inside their 2026-11-16 ranges. The idle cycle count moved from 58.0 to 56.8 per tick, just outside its range (57.5 to 58.1).
