# Board run: D2 CPU merging

Status: partly run on CM5 2026-11-10 (per-stage times taken; the instruction count was not, valgrind is not on the board).

## Check

- Per-stage overhead of a chain of CPU nodes, merged and unmerged (`Config { merge: false }`). `dev`
  measured 498 ns merged and 655 ns unmerged per stage.
- The 16-stage idle tick in instructions, against `dev`'s 27,064 (`origin/dev`). Commands: `daedalus-budget-harness chain` and `daedalus-budget-harness idle`, with `valgrind --tool=callgrind` for the instruction count.
- Merged must be below unmerged, and neither may be worse than `dev`. A number that gets worse is
  reported with the reason.

## Record

Commit, kernel, CPU governor, both per-stage times and the instruction count, next to the `dev`
figures above.

## Result


Run on 2026-11-10 on the CM5 (raze2): OS image 2026.2.0-dev.993+e1097ec7, kernel 7.2.9-v8-16k, aarch64, 4 cores, CPU governor `performance` at 2.4 GHz. Tested commit: `next` at bdbfe947.

From the budget harness's `ticks` bench (the C1 record above), per stage with one feed and one take per tick included:

| Form | CM5, `next` | Per stage | `dev` (CM5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| merged (`fused_chain_16_tick`) | 1.0741 µs | 67.1 ns | 498 ns |
| unmerged (`unfused_chain_16_tick`) | 1.8428 µs | 115.2 ns | 655 ns |

Merged is below unmerged on this board: yes. Neither figure is worse than the `dev` figure recorded for the CM5. `dev` was not re-run on this board, so this is a recorded comparison, not a verified one.

Instruction count of the 16-stage idle tick: not run. valgrind is not on the board, so the count (27,064 on `dev`) cannot be taken here. The idle tick's wall time on this board is 33.42 ns; that is not comparable to an instruction count.
