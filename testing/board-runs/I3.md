# Board run: I3 large-value handoff timings

Status: re-run 2026-11-11 on CM5 (raze2) at `next` 6245c925: the six handoff rows run and are timed. The allocation rows and the RX 6800 XT run were not made, and the dev comparison is not done here.

## Check

Build from NVMe, never from the HDD.

- `cargo bench -p daedalus-budget-harness --bench ticks -- handoff`: the six `handoff_*` rows,
  a megabyte read by 8 nodes and handed on through 8 stages, as a handle, as shared bytes and
  as a `Vec<u8>`. Record each on both machines.
- Compare with `dev` at commit 36d2af6 (`docs/benchmarks-log.md` on `origin/dev`: the frame handoff
  overhead, `SharedFrame` against `SharedBytes` against `Vec<u8>`, both paths), at the sizes
  used there. The graphs are not the same graphs, so say which rows are compared.
- `scripts/ci.sh budgets` on each machine: the counted rows are the same everywhere. A row
  that differs is a finding.

## Result


Run on 2026-11-10 on the CM5 (raze2): OS image 2026.2.0-dev.993+e1097ec7, kernel 7.2.9-v8-16k, aarch64, 4 cores, CPU governor `performance` at 2.4 GHz. Tested commit: `next` at bdbfe947.

- `cargo bench -p daedalus-budget-harness --bench ticks -- handoff` on the board (`ticks-bench`), the six `handoff_*` rows, median of criterion's three figures (low, median, high in brackets):

| Row | CM5, `next` (median) | Range |
|---|---|---|
| `handoff_handle_read_tick` | 1.178 us | 1.177 to 1.179 us |
| `handoff_handle_chain_tick` | 8.80 us | 8.31 to 9.17 us |
| `handoff_shared_read_tick` | 1.181 us | 1.181 to 1.182 us |
| `handoff_shared_chain_tick` | 2.72 us | 2.68 to 2.77 us |
| `handoff_vec_read_tick` | 1.181 us | 1.179 to 1.182 us |
| `handoff_vec_chain_tick` | 4.319 ms | 4.296 to 4.344 ms |

On this board the handle chain is about 3 times slower than the shared chain (8.80 us against 2.72 us). The handle reads are equal at about 1.18 us across all three paths. This is an observation from the run, not diagnosed.

- Comparison with `dev` at 36d2af6. The frame-handoff figures are in that commit's message, not in `docs/benchmarks-log.md` on `dev` at that commit. They were taken on a Ryzen 9 5900X with a 1920x1080 NV12 frame of 3,110,400 bytes: SharedFrame 0.94 us static and 1.54 us stable; SharedBytes 0.97 us and 1.58 us; Vec<u8> 55 us static and 1.44 ms stable. The rows above are on a different machine, at the sizes and graphs the procedure describes, so no row is compared directly with those figures.
- Allocations per tick, from `ex-daedalus-budget-harness allocations`: `alloc_handoff_handle_read_tick` 0; `alloc_handoff_handle_chain_tick` 8; `alloc_handoff_shared_read_tick` 0; `alloc_handoff_shared_chain_tick` 8; `alloc_handoff_vec_read_tick` 0; `alloc_handoff_vec_chain_tick` 16.

Not run: the RX 6800 XT run (a different machine); `scripts/ci.sh budgets` on the CM5 (the board has no cargo, and the script builds on the host). The counted rows were taken on the board only, so the statement that they are the same everywhere is not checked.
